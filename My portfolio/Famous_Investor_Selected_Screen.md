# Famous Investor Selected Screen

## Summary
This screen selects the most attractive ideas from the portfolios of famous investors, with a focus on durable earnings power, strong balance sheets, and rational valuation. The names below are not random picks; they are chosen because they repeatedly show up in high-conviction owner-investor portfolios and match the quality and moat principles that matter in long-duration investing.

## Most attractive names

### 1) Microsoft (MSFT)
- Why it stands out: cash generation, cloud scale, productivity moat, AI monetization
- Thesis: Microsoft has one of the best long-duration compounding platforms in the market.
- Kill condition: If Azure growth stalls materially or AI monetization fails to convert into sustainable margin expansion, reduce or exit.

### 2) Amazon (AMZN)
- Why it stands out: AWS + ads + retail efficiency + AI upside
- Thesis: Amazon can compound for years if operating leverage continues to improve.
- Kill condition: If AWS slows materially or retail economics stop improving, trim exposure.

### 3) Alphabet (GOOGL)
- Why it stands out: search moat, cloud investment, AI optionality
- Thesis: Alphabet combines sheer scale with durable monetization and balance-sheet strength.
- Kill condition: If search share weakens or AI monetization does not translate into pricing power, reduce.

### 4) Meta (META)
- Why it stands out: ad engine quality and free cash flow generation
- Thesis: Meta has large operating leverage and can continue converting growth into earnings power.
- Kill condition: If digital advertising slows materially or AI investment compresses profit margins, exit or cut.

### 5) NVIDIA (NVDA)
- Why it stands out: AI infrastructure leader and pricing power
- Thesis: NVIDIA is in the center of the AI buildout and benefits from critical compute demand.
- Kill condition: If hyperscale capex slows, margins compress, or valuation detaches from future earnings, reduce.

### 6) Apple (AAPL)
- Why it stands out: installed-base economics, services, high cash flow
- Thesis: Apple is a durable compounding machine with strong recurring revenue characteristics.
- Kill condition: If hardware demand weakens and services growth stalls, reduce.

### 7) Visa (V)
- Why it stands out: network moat, low capital intensity, pricing power
- Thesis: Visa is a classic high-quality payments franchise.
- Kill condition: If volumes stall or valuation loses support, trim.

### 8) JPMorgan (JPM)
- Why it stands out: excellent bank quality and capital structure
- Thesis: JPM is one of the strongest financial institutions in the market.
- Kill condition: If credit stress worsens materially, reduce.

### 9) Costco (COST)
- Why it stands out: customer loyalty, membership economics, defensive cash flow
- Thesis: Costco is a strong compounder with resilient demand and low volatility.
- Kill condition: If membership churn rises or pricing discipline breaks, reduce.

### 10) Exxon (XOM)
- Why it stands out: cash generation, inflation hedge, disciplined capital return
- Thesis: XOM offers steady free cash flow and a constructive energy cycle.
- Kill condition: If commodity weakness persists and cash generation collapses, exit.

### 11) TSMC (TSM)
- Why it stands out: strategic semiconductor leader and AI infrastructure exposure
- Thesis: TSMC is crucial to the global semiconductor supply chain.
- Kill condition: If semiconductor demand weakens materially or geopolitics creates significant downside, reduce.

### 12) NRG / UBER / CRWV
- These are more tactical ideas: attractive when cyclical or thematic conditions are favorable.
- They are not core holdings in the same way as MSFT, AMZN, AAPL, or V.

## Screening insight
The strongest ideas are the ones that overlap across multiple famous investor portfolios:
- MSFT
- AMZN
- GOOGL
- AAPL
- V
- JPM
- COST
- XOM
- CVX
- META

These names provide the cleanest blend of quality, moat, valuation discipline, and compounding potential.
