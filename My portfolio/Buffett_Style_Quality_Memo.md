# Buffett-Style Quality Value Portfolio

## Inspiration
This portfolio is inspired by Warren Buffett and Charlie Munger’s philosophy:
- buy strong, durable businesses
- prefer high-quality management and moats
- hold for years rather than months
- do not overpay for growth
- keep a cash reserve for opportunities

## Selected stocks
- AAPL – large recurring cash generation, ecosystem moat
- MSFT – software and cloud platform with durable margins
- COST – strong customer retention and low-cost operating model
- KO – classic consumer franchise with pricing power
- JPM – banking quality and capital strength
- V – payment network and structural pricing power
- XOM – energy cash flow and value cycle exposure
- OXY – value-oriented commodity cash generation
- BAC – high-quality financial franchise
- C – value play within financials
- PG – defensive consumer staple with steady cash flow
- CASH – optionality and downside protection

## Portfolio thesis
The idea is not to chase hype. It is to own a compressed basket of businesses that are economically strong, financially disciplined, and capable of compounding over long periods. This style intentionally favors predictable earnings, balance-sheet strength, and reasonable valuation relative to intrinsic value.

## Kill condition
A holding is reduced or sold if:
- the business loses its competitive edge
- earnings quality deteriorates materially
- the valuation becomes too expensive relative to future cash flow
- management destroys capital or balance-sheet quality weakens

## Role in the portfolio
This is a long-horizon, lower-turnover portfolio built to compound steadily while preserving resilience during downturns.
